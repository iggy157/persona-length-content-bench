# Raw data sources

## IPIP-NEO 300 facet key (`ipip/`)
- International Personality Item Pool, public domain. https://ipip.ori.org/newNEOFacetsKey.htm (fetched 2026-10-21).
- 30 facets x 10 items, each item marked `+ keyed` or `- keyed`. The personas are the + keyed items (strong pole) or the - keyed items
  (weak pole) of one facet, rewritten in the second person (`pcb.textnorm.to_second_person`).
